Principles

1

The slot is the brand

The capsule shape of a time slot shows up everywhere: buttons, tags, the logo. People learn it in one booking.

2

Cyan means go

Cyan marks the one thing to tap. Everything else stays black, white and soft grey, so the next step is obvious.

3

Black is the stage

The hero and owner panels are black so cyan glows on them. Cyan is a highlight, never a background for whole sections. One sticker or key word per screen.

Type

A bold, wide display face for big moments, a clean sans for everything you read, and mono for times and prices.

Text, buttons, labels and forms. 400 to 700.

05

Patterns

Five quiet backgrounds made from the shapes of time. One per screen, always behind content.

Use
  • Orbit or glow behind a hero.
  • Fading grid for empty states.
  • Hours and hatch inside the agenda.
Avoid
  • Two patterns on one screen.
  • Text directly on a pattern without a surface.
  • Glow inside the owner dashboard.
06

Motion

A little bounce when you choose something, calm everywhere else. All motion stops for people who prefer reduced motion.

07

UX rules

Branch→Professional→Services→One time→Name + phone

One visit, one time

One professional does every chosen service. The client picks one start time and sees the total duration and price.

Only show what is free

Never show a time and then reject it. Dim days with no openings and open on the first free day.

No account for clients

Ask for a name and phone at the very end, then hand over a private booking link.

No surprises with money

Deposit now, amount later and total stay visible, with the currency on every price.

Today first

The owner dashboard opens on today. Only things that need action get color.

Made for thumbs

Slots at least 44px tall, the main button at the bottom on phones, nothing scrolls sideways.
